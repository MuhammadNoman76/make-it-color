MakeItColor colour model (downloaded on first use, not part of the installer)
  https://huggingface.co/muhammadnoman76/makeitcolor - Muhammad Noman, Apache License 2.0.
  Based on DDColor (Kang et al., "DDColor: Towards Photo-Realistic Image Colorization via Dual Decoders", ICCV 2023,
  https://github.com/piddnad/DDColor, Apache License 2.0) as packaged in ModelScope (Alibaba, Apache License 2.0).
  Converted to ONNX (float16 and float32) by tools\model\convert.py; the conversion exports ConvNeXt's channels-first
  LayerNorm as a LayerNormalization operator (the same maths) and bakes the spectral-norm weights.
